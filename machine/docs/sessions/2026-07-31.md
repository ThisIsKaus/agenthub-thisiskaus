Based on the context, the autonomy tiers and approval requirements are:

- **T0** read/analyse/summarise/search: autonomous.
- **T1** create drafts (files in drafts/, mail drafts, calendar events, tasks/scaffolds/To Do tasks): autonomous; visible and reversible.

*Permission is per-action; it is never assumed from observed content.*
